Consignment Program Policy

Onboarding & Item Eligibility
Eligible Items & Condition
  • Condition: Must be Discogs VG (Very Good) grade or above
  • Formats: Sealed items and standard LPs are accepted. Singles are not accepted
  • Uniqueness: The store only accepts unique records; the portal checks for duplicates against existing inventory
  • Not Accepted: Counterfeits/bootlegs, rare/high-value items requiring appraisal
  • Store Discretion: The store may reject any item deemed unsellable or undesirable at either the portal submission stage or the in-store check-in
Submission Process
  • Consignor signs up and submits items via the online portal
  • Consignor brings the physical records to the store for final inspection
Volume Maximum number of items is determined by store capacity; there is no minimum
Pricing, Sales & Commission
Pricing & Reductions
  • Initial Price: Set by consignor as a ratio (0.7 to 1.1) of the Discogs suggested price
  • Price Reductions: No price changes will be suggested before the end of the initial 90-day term. After the term, if a price change is suggested, the consignor must either approve the change or pick up the item as unsold
  • Markdowns: No markdowns for cosmetic issues; these should be accounted for in the initial grading and price
Commission & Payout
  • Rate: Variable commission based on store inventory: 10% at ≤70% capacity, linearly increasing to 40% at 110% capacity. Consignors see the current commission rate when checking in records via the online portal.
  • Incentive: A +5% commission bonus is offered for choosing store credit (e.g., 15% instead of 10%)
  • Payout: Available upon request via the portal once balance meets the $10.00 minimum. If an account is closed, the consignor may request a final payout of any amount
Sales Reporting Consignors monitor all sales and inventory status in real-time through the online portal. No separate reports are issued
Intake, Liability & Item Care
Check-in & Verification
  • Consignor self-grades items in the portal; the store verifies the grading during the physical check-in
  • A barcode is assigned to each record for tracking
Liability & Disputes
  • Store Responsibility: Covers missing items, visible damage missed at intake, burglary, natural disasters, and shoplifting
  • Consignor Responsibility: Invisible defects (e.g., skips) are assumed pre-existing; the affected record is returned to the consignor as unsold
Cleaning & Services Records should be clean at intake. The store offers a cleaning service at a discounted rate, billed separately
Unsold Items & End-of-Term
Consignment Period
  • Initial Term: 90 days at the consignor's set price
  • Extension: Can be extended for another 90 days at a store-determined price with a new contract
Unsold Item Process
  • After a record is taken from the shelves (for any reason), the consignor has 30 days to pick it up
  • The store sends weekly email reminders. Items not picked up within 30 days become store property
  • Early Removal: Consignors may remove individual records or terminate the entire contract early at any time without consequence via the online portal; the store will take the items off the shelves for pickup, treating them as unsold/returned
Consignor Responsibilities & Systems
Consignor Duties
  • Certify legal ownership in the contract
  • Provide accurate grading and information
  • Keep contact information current to receive notifications
  • Pick up unsold/returned items within the 30-day deadline
Technology & System
  • Online Portal: Provides login for real-time inventory tracking, sales history, payout requests, and early item removal
  • Notifications: Via email
Legal & Administrative
Agreement Details
  • Contract length is 90 days
  • The store provides a receipt of sales for tax purposes
